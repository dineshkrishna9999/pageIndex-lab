# PageIndex Demo Report

This small report exists to demonstrate how PageIndex reads a document and builds a tree index.

The company had a strong year. Revenue increased because of higher subscription sales and better customer retention.

This report is intentionally short and factual. Each section contains a few facts that are easy to verify after indexing.

The report uses headings so PageIndex can compare the document structure with the actual page content.

# Executive Summary

The 2026 plan focused on three goals: grow recurring revenue, improve reliability, and reduce support response time.

The most important result was annual revenue of $1.2 million, compared with $900,000 in 2025. Customer retention also improved from 88% to 92%.

The operating plan also included better monitoring, clearer ownership for incidents, and a monthly review of customer feedback.

The finance team measured progress using revenue, gross margin, operating profit, retention, and response time.

# Quarterly Results

## First Quarter

First-quarter revenue was $250,000. The team released the new reporting dashboard and signed 18 new customers.

The first-quarter focus was product visibility. The dashboard gave customers a simple view of usage, billing, and service health.

The team also documented the first version of the support process so that customer questions could be routed consistently.

## Second Quarter

Second-quarter revenue was $275,000. Cloud costs increased by 8%, but gross margin remained stable at 72%.

During the second quarter, the company reviewed infrastructure usage and removed several unused test environments.

This reduced wasted capacity and helped offset part of the increase in cloud pricing.

## Third Quarter

Third-quarter revenue was $300,000. Customer retention reached 92%, and average support response time fell to 4 hours.

The support improvement came from a small knowledge base and a daily review of unresolved customer requests.

The product team also added clearer error messages, which reduced repeated support questions.

## Fourth Quarter

Fourth-quarter revenue was $375,000. The company finished the year with annual revenue of $1.2 million and operating profit of $180,000.

The fourth quarter was the strongest quarter of the year. New subscriptions and renewals both exceeded the internal forecast.

Management plans to keep the same reporting format next year so that performance can be compared quarter by quarter.

# Risks and Next Steps

The main risks are dependence on one cloud provider, a small support team, and rising infrastructure costs.

Next year, management plans to add a second cloud provider, hire two support engineers, and keep gross margin above 70%.

The cloud-provider project will begin with a small backup workload. The team will measure reliability, cost, and recovery time before moving more traffic.

The support hiring plan is intended to preserve the four-hour response target as the customer base grows.

# Glossary

Revenue means money earned from customers. Gross margin is revenue minus direct delivery costs, divided by revenue. Customer retention is the percentage of customers who continue using the service.
